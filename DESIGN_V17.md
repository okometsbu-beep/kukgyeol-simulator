# v17 Design Reset · Steam reference synthesis

This reset treats the old build as content inventory, not as a design constraint. The target is a replayable international-relationship life RPG with mystery as one viable strategy rather than the entire genre.

## Reference games and the mechanic worth studying

| Game | Concrete loop | Why the loop works | Principle used here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monster Prom | Spend a short, fixed number of weeks in locations, raise stats, trigger events, pass stat checks, pursue a date | Runs are short; every action has opportunity cost; hundreds of events make reruns diverge | Fixed pre-marriage deadline, repeated weekly allocation, character/event variety |
| I Was a Teenage Exocolonist | Choose one activity each month, grow skills/relationships, turn memories into cards, reach one of many endings, replay with knowledge of past lives | Failure creates information and tools for the next run instead of deleting its value | Persistent ending history and NG+ `past-run review` action |
| Volcano Princess | Spend action points on study, work, socializing and exploration across a finite calendar, then resolve major milestones and endings | Several desirable tracks compete for the same time budget | Relationship, livelihood, language and insight share the same weekly slot |
| Growing Up / Chinese Parents | Schedule activities, manage mood/resources, raise skills, hit exams/milestones, inherit advantages into a new generation | A clear repeating turn makes a long life legible and strategic | Weekly plan is the primary repeatable verb; large decisions use accumulated preparation |
| The Life and Suffering of Sir Brante | Make chapter choices, build stats/willpower/relationships, later choices appear only if earlier requirements were met | Early decisions visibly change what is possible much later | Marriage proposal is gated by earlier life preparation instead of only a final roll |
| Love Is All Around | First-person character scenes, frequent relationship choices, affection gates, hidden branches and multiple endings | High character appeal and immediate audiovisual feedback keep a simple system engaging | Preserve partner-specific episodes and make system choices return to character scenes |
| Citizen Sleeper | At the start of each cycle, assign a limited pool of dice/actions among survival, money and people; clocks keep moving | The player can rarely do everything, so narrative choices are also resource choices | Time is a hard currency and recovery competes with progress |

## New core loop

1. Story scene exposes a person, promise, problem or opportunity.
2. A weekly-plan turn asks where the next block of time goes.
3. The chosen investment changes one long-term preparation track and immediate relationship/economy state.
4. A milestone scene tests what was built, not just what dialogue option was clicked.
5. Marriage opens the existing long-life layer: wedding, housing, work, remittance, intimacy, children, conflict and finance.
6. Ending history unlocks a replay-only planning action so failure becomes knowledge.

The four preparation tracks are deliberately incomplete on their own:

- `Bond`: emotional history and partner trust.
- `Living`: money and practical independence.
- `Language`: communication without outsourcing every nuance.
- `Insight`: evidence, verification and uncertainty management.

Rest is a fifth action with no permanent track gain. It exists so stress is a real scheduling problem instead of a number the player fixes after the interesting decisions are over.

## Design rules from v17 onward

- A new feature must strengthen the weekly loop, a milestone, or a replay consequence. Menu-only complexity is not progress.
- Major decisions should be gated or reshaped by earlier play at least as often as they are decided by a final check.
- Investigation cannot be the universal best answer. Information has time, money and relationship costs.
- Partner-specific content should return after system choices so the game never turns into a spreadsheet with a dating skin.
- A failed run should reveal useful information or a new strategic option for the next run.
- Content scale is still the largest gap to premium Steam games. Framework improvements do not count as equivalent to hundreds of handcrafted events, bespoke art, audio, QA and human playtesting.
